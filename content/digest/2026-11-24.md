### Jensen Huang is back as Nvidia CEO after Trump lobbies the board; shares add over $1T

Nvidia's board voted unanimously on Monday to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO, 11 days after he resigned (Nov. 12) over reported affairs with a direct report and with Alice Huang, an executive at Megaspeed, the Singapore company under investigation over alleged GPU exports to China. The vote came six days after chair Harvey Jones ruled out rehiring him; the board said 'senior levels' of the U.S. government weighed in. President Trump told CNBC he had been 'speaking to the board directly and indirectly', and TLDR reports he made a personal promise that Huang had not broken national security laws; the post-resignation stock drop and questions about the interim CEO's leadership also drove the decision. Interim CEO Colette Kress stays on as CFO. Nvidia shares jumped over 30% after the open and added over $1T in market value, which The Rundown calls the largest single-day gain in stock-market history. Why it matters: the case is far from closed. Huang faces a House hearing on Dec. 1 (Hakeem Jeffries called the move 'indefensible, irresponsible, and incomprehensible'), TLDR Hardware reports U.S. intelligence tracked Alice Huang's contacts with an officer of China's Ministry of State Security from spring 2025 until this fall (Trump was briefed at least twice), and The Deep View links a report accusing Huang of decades-long abusive behavior toward staff. For robotics, his return eases fears of a pullback: Nvidia has backed 35+ robotics and physical-AI startups (Figure, Neura, Skild, Wayve), and its NVentures arm called at least three portfolio startups to reassure them while he was out.

## Models & Launches

### Tencent's 770B-parameter Hy4 and Z.ai's looped GLM-5.4 push open-weight models forward

Tencent released open-weight Hy4, which understands text, images and video with a 770B-parameter backbone that activates 49B parameters per token, plus million-token context, sparse attention and a speculative-decoding drafter; it targets coding, computer use, finance, research and long-running assistants. Z.ai's GLM-5.4 is its first 'loop transformer': the 750B model runs the same 28-layer block twice, getting more compute per token without more parameters; Z.ai shows CAD and video-generation demos and admits it can still spend too long reasoning. Nvidia released Nemotron-Labs-Sigma, 14 open 3B and 8B checkpoints that generate text by denoising (diffusion) instead of one token at a time, with the 8B reaching a 3.46x average throughput speedup over autoregressive decoding on a GB200. Why it matters: open models are getting bigger and more efficient at the same time.
